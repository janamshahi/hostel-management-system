About DIDI BAHINI

More Than Just a Place to Stay

DIDI BAHINI GIRLS HOSTEL is a residential facility dedicated to providing girls and students with a safe, clean, comfortable and friendly place to stay.

We believe that a good living environment plays an important role in a student's academic and personal development. The hostel encourages discipline, cooperation, responsibility and mutual respect among residents.

Our aim is to provide students with a supportive home-like environment where they can concentrate on their education while enjoying a positive residential experience.

  • Safe and peaceful residential environment
  • Comfortable accommodation for students
  • Clean and organized living environment
  • Student-friendly atmosphere
  • Focus on discipline and mutual respect

Hostel Guidelines

Responsible Living

Every resident is expected to contribute to a safe, respectful and peaceful hostel environment.

Maintain cleanliness and hygiene in rooms and common areas.
Respect other residents and maintain a friendly environment.
Follow hostel timings and administrative instructions.
Take proper care of hostel rooms, furniture and facilities.
Maintain a peaceful environment suitable for study.
Inform hostel administration about important issues when necessary.
Follow safety guidelines and responsible residential practices.
Maintain mutual respect and cooperation among residents.
